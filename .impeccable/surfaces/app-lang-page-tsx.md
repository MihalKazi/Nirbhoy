# Surface brief: whole site (Next.js app/[lang])

Mode: Operate (survivor picks her situation, follows a plan, uses tools: proof vault, GD draft, helplines). Stories/about are Read.

Audience: woman in Bangladesh facing tech-facilitated abuse, on a phone, frightened, often at night. No invented stories, stats, counts. Sentinel Desks = proposed pilot.
Constraints: bn + en equal; on-device data only; quick exit; tel: links; code-led (no image generation, illustrations authored as inline SVG). User rejected: orange/black, stripes, cardboard, heavy borders, condensed caps, sage tea-garden (too plain), and every rolled direction. User PINNED: "Illustrated & warm", purple/lavender, user-friendly, innovative-looking.
Memorable moment: the hero illustration of a Bangladeshi woman sitting safely under a lavender arch of light, phone in hand; plan steps fill violet-to-green as she moves.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A warm, illustrated safe room in lavender: she is met by a drawn woman like her, sitting calmly under an arch of light, and every screen speaks softly and plainly. Refuses the cold helpline grid, the alarm banner and every costume tried before.
OWN-WORLD: Lavender-white ground #faf7fd, white rounded cards (20px), soft lavender tint #efe8fa, deep plum ink #2a1f3d, violet #6b46c1 for action, peach #f7d9cc for warmth, green #2e7d5b only for done, red #c62f3f only for emergency. Pill buttons. Anek Bangla, sentence case. Flat hand-authored SVG illustrations: faceless Bangladeshi women (warm brown skin, salwar kameez + orna, one in hijab), 5-6 flat tones, no outlines.
STORY: She sees a woman like her, safe; reads one calm question; picks her situation or the fallback; follows numbered steps; leaves with a plan, proof, a GD draft and a number called.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone: header (brand, menu with text size inside it, language, quick exit); small hero illustration (170px); headline 'আপনার সঙ্গে কী হচ্ছে?'; reassurance; red emergency card and first situation within ~700px; 1-2-3 strip moves below the list. Desktop: rail left (with text size), hero text + illustration top, situations in one column with a tools aside (cited adaptation: tools stay one tap away without competing with the situation list).
FORM: User-pinned brief (illustrated, warm, lavender) overriding the de37876c roll after rounds were declined.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
