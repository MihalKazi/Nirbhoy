# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (user-chosen). Bilingual: Bangla + English, user-owned build. Mobile-first: survivors arrive on phones.

## Users

Primary: women and girls in Bangladesh (and allies acting for them) hit by technology-facilitated gender-based violence (TFGBV): harassment, blackmail, doxxing, deepfakes, AI image abuse. They arrive in crisis, usually on a phone, frightened, needing to know what to do right now: preserve evidence, file a GD, reach the right agency or helpline. Secondary (unconfirmed): young women/students seeking prevention knowledge; hackathon judges evaluating the concept.

## Product Purpose

Survivor-first TFGBV support platform for Bangladesh, built for the DKC Digital Respect & Cohesion Fellowship 2026 (track: gender-based online violence). Helps a survivor (1) record their experience, (2) preserve evidence correctly (chain of custody), (3) generate a draft police GD, (4) find emergency cyber and legal aid contacts, (5) see stories of resilience. Success: a survivor leaves with preserved evidence, a ready GD draft, and the right number to call. Fellowship context: 8-month project, 8 divisions, seed 50,000 BDT, deadline 3 Oct 2026.

## Positioning

Bangla-first, TFGBV-specific evidence-to-complaint pipeline: evidence guide plus automated GD draft (targeting gd.police.gov.bd) plus a curated Bangladesh agency directory in one flow. Generic helplines list numbers; this walks a survivor from first screenshot to filed complaint.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Evidence guide rules: do NOT block accounts before collecting evidence, do NOT delete messages/comments, do NOT deactivate own ID. Steps: copy URL; uncropped screenshot; screen-record (including click-through from post to offender profile); archive via archive.ph / web.archive.org or Save as PDF; back up originals (Drive, email attachment, pendrive) to keep metadata.
- GD generator fields: main instigator profile/page/group + direct link; list of main attackers (death/rape/harm threats, links); screenshots + screen recording; attack type (doxxing, deepfake/edited media, offline physical-safety risk).
- Directory (verify numbers before ship): PCSW 01320-000888; CID Cyber Police Centre 01320-010148 / 010146 / 01769-691522; DMP Cyber Crime 01320-000888; DMP Victim Support QRT 01320-042055; National emergency 999; Women & Children helpline 109; National Legal Aid 16699/16430; BTRC 100; ASK 01724-415677; BLAST 01715-220220; BNWLA 01711-880777; Bangladesh Mahila Parishad 02-9553849; BRAC HRLS 02-8831291; Kaan Pete Roi 09612-119911 (screenshot shows 01779-554391, conflicting; verify).
- Disclaimer (required, Bangla): platform is an experimental pilot; structure, info, and technical features will be refined.
- Campus "Digital Sentinel Desks" (screenshot: DU, Eden Mohila College, Jagannath University, StopNCII hashing, "30 active sentinels") is a PROPOSED pilot, not real: label as proposed, never state counts as fact.
- Privacy is a hard constraint: sensitive data should stay on-device; no server storage of evidence unless decided.
- Both languages required.

## Product Principles

1. Survivor first: nothing between a scared person and the next action.
2. Evidence before emotion: preserving proof correctly comes before any reaction step.
3. Trust through honesty: pilot labels, verified numbers, no invented claims.
4. Bangla and English equal citizens.
5. Safety and privacy by default; never shame, never blame.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Mobile-first, low-bandwidth friendly, Bangla typography rendered properly, tap-to-call links, high contrast and calm state for users in distress, plain language.
